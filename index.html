Research Summary

My research focuses on Scientific Machine Learning with emphasis on Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs), compressible flows with shocks, multiscale turbulent flow modeling, and GPU-accelerated high-performance computing. I develop data-free and hybrid attention-based frameworks for computational fluid dynamics.

Data-Free PINNs

Solving flow problems from the governing equations alone, without training data.

Shocks & Compressible Flow

Robust physics-informed models for shocks and shock–boundary layer interactions.

Multiscale Turbulence

Attention-based and multiscale networks that capture long-range dependencies.

Turbomachinery

Turbo-PINN, wake prediction in low-pressure turbines, and LES of the SPLEEN cascade.

GPU & HPC

underPINN, a modular JAX-based framework, and GPU-accelerated simulation.
